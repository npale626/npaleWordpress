Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical project take?

Timing depends on the site scope, content readiness and any required integrations. We can discuss a schedule after reviewing your requirements.

Do I need to provide content?

Content responsibilities are agreed as part of the project scope. You can provide your copy and images, and we can discuss any additional content support you may need.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

WordPress provides tools for managing site content. The editing approach and any handover or training can be discussed and agreed for your project.

What about hosting and domains?

Hosting and domain requirements depend on your project. We can discuss suitable options and clarify account ownership before work begins.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Maintenance and support can be discussed separately and quoted based on the services and response needs you require.
